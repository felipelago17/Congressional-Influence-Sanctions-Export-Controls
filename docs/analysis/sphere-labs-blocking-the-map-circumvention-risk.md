## What is reported (summary)

A payments-compliance vendor argues that country-list screening misses export-control circumvention via redomiciling, intermediaries and remote access, and that payment flows (recurring lease payments, financing ahead of capacity, payer and end-user mismatch) can reveal diversion earlier than customs data. It uses Megaspeed as its example and cites the 16 Nov 2026 BIS remote-access rule.
