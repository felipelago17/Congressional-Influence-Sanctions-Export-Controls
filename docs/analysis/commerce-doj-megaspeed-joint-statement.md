## Why this note exists

The [Nvidia CEO resignation note](nvidia-ceo-resignation-export-controls.md) recorded the DOJ-Megaspeed matter as "unconfirmed by any agency document read" and listed `megaspeed-doj-probe-2026-11` as needing a DOJ/BIS document. The joint statement is that document. It is an **agency response to a congressional-and-press pressure cycle**, so it is recorded here as an executive-side signal, not as an enforcement action (nothing has been charged).

| Item | Statement (paraphrased) | Source tier |

## 2. What it does not settle (in the portions read)

- The **Nvidia general counsel's August 2025 contact** with BIS agents and the question of who authorised it (NYT, press only).
- The **Entity List**: four other agencies reportedly pushed for listing; Commerce has not acted (FT, press only). No Federal Register notice exists.
- **Megaspeed's ownership** and any link to Chinese government-owned parent companies, the subject of the October 2025 Cotton request.

## 3. Congressional signals this responds to (aggregated by party and committee)

- **Senate Republican (letter to Commerce, c. Oct 2025):** requested an investigation into Megaspeed's ownership, affiliations and US ties. Predates the statement by about a year; no agency reply is recorded.
- **House Democrats (reported 10 Nov):** pressed the Commerce Secretary to say what his officials knew about Nvidia's contact (press only).
- **Senate Democrats (12 Nov letters):** asked whether Megaspeed has been blocked from buying Nvidia chips and sought an investigation update and document preservation (see the Nvidia note).
- **Executive-branch friction (press):** reports that the Treasury Secretary said Commerce was late on restrictions, and that the President acknowledged tension between the two departments (Alabama Daily News; original not read).

## 4. Assessment against the repository's framework

1. **Linkage grade: `proximity-only`.** The statement does not cite any member letter, hearing or bill. It was issued days after oversight letters and press scrutiny, but **no inference of causation is drawn**. A `documented-request` grade would need an agency reply citing a congressional request.
2. **It is a Tier 2 source.** Per the [source hierarchy](../methodology/source-hierarchy.md) it can support a `proximity-only` entry but not a stronger grade on its own.
3. **It shifts the oversight question.** With the timeline official, the open congressional questions narrow to (a) the 2025 contact between Nvidia and BIS leadership, (b) why no Entity List action followed, and (c) the interagency split. All are **oversight** levers rather than legislative ones. See [Administration Levers](../influence/administration-levers.md).
4. **Legislative hook unchanged.** The statement does not alter the FY2027 NDAA picture: remote-access and chip-security language still depend on conference and White House support (see the [NDAA note](fy27-ndaa-ai-chip-export-bills.md)).
5. **Timing.** The new Democratic House majority arrives 3 Jan 2027; oversight of BIS decisions on Megaspeed is a plausible early subject, but that is a forward-looking observation, not a sourced finding.
