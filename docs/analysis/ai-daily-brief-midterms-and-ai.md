## Argument as billed

The Democratic wave is expected to bring new scrutiny to AI on three fronts: data-centre construction (local permitting, energy and water), AI regulation, and the emerging 2028 presidential field's positioning on AI. The host's stated thesis is that the backlash is more likely to produce *better terms for host communities* than to halt the build-out.

## Why it is filed here

- **Data-centre siting and chip controls intersect.** Where compute is built, and under what conditions, is the domestic face of the same question export controls address abroad: who gets access to advanced compute. Congressional pressure on domestic siting could show up alongside remote-access and chip-security bills in the same committees.
- **Oversight capacity is fungible.** The oversight gains all post-midterm sources agree on ([cross-source check](midterms-2026-cross-source-check.md)) can be spent on AI hearings as readily as on sanctions waivers; which gets priority is itself a signal worth tracking.
