## What is reported

- The **Dutch Foreign Ministry** told Politico its main concern with the MATCH Act is extraterritoriality: each state decides its own national-security and export-control policy. The bill's reach over Dutch firms was called "unacceptable between partners" (via The Next Web).
- The bill, in press summaries, gives allies including the Netherlands and Japan **150 days** to align limits on the sale and servicing of advanced chipmaking equipment in China, failing which **Commerce must extend US controls unilaterally** to equipment from those countries.
- It is reported to cover more DUV tools and servicing of installed machines in China; **ASML has criticised it**; IO+ says China is nearly a third of ASML's revenue.
- The Dutch government was **already opposed in May 2026** (IO+); the new statement is a renewed public position, not a first.
