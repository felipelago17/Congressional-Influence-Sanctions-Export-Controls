## Points relevant to this repository

- **Oversight / subpoenas.** Committee control passes to Democrats; the Senate Permanent Subcommittee on Investigations is expected to be chaired by Sen. Blumenthal, who has said publicly he intends to use subpoena power. The article notes subpoenas can be litigated for months or years, and criminal referrals go to a DOJ run by the administration — i.e. oversight is a *cost-raising* lever, not a *reversal* lever.
- **Confirmations.** Senate majority can block or delay nominees (cabinet, ambassadors, judges). Relevant to Treasury/OFAC, Commerce/BIS and State appointments — track under the **nominee holds** lever.
- **Veto arithmetic.** Bills need both chambers plus either presidential assent or a two-thirds override. Same constraint identified in the [Sanctions Drop](sanctions-drop-18-democrats-take-congress.md) and [Dow Jones](dj-risk-journal-tweaks-not-major-change.md) notes.
- **Tariffs.** Reversal would need House passage plus assent or override; experts quoted expect slow price effects even if tariffs fall. Consistent with the "tweaks, not major change" consensus.
- **Timing.** New majorities take office 3 January 2027. Everything before that is the lame-duck 119th Congress (NDAA, RASA sine-die risk).
