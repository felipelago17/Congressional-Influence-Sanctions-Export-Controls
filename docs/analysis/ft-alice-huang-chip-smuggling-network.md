## What is reported (summary)

The FT reports that Alice Huang, a former Megaspeed executive at the centre of US investigations, was connected to a web of companies (an OEM server partner formerly tied to an Entity-Listed firm, a gaming group, a Shanghai distributor, and a Cambodia-based figure US authorities link to the sanctioned Prince Group) that moved Nvidia chips to China-based users. Some US officials are said to suspect her of being a Chinese spy, which Beijing calls a "smear". Declared imports into Megaspeed's Malaysian subsidiary were about $4.6bn in the year to December 2025. Nvidia says it block-listed Megaspeed and a subsidiary in 2025 and restricted sales to the OEM partner. The report ties into the administration's remote-access rule, announced the same week. The full corporate-structure detail is in the [sibling repository's case note](https://github.com/felipelago17/Sanctions-and-Export-Controls-AI-and-Oil).
