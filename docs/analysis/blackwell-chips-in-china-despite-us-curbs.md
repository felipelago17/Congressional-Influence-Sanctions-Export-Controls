## What is reported (summary)

Bloomberg reported on 27 Nov 2026 that a GB300 rack and a GB200 server, identified from Chinese loan filings and a public warranty database, were installed at a Shanghai data-centre project linked to Alice Huang's network; Bloomberg says it could not establish how the hardware reached China or whether Megaspeed diverted it. Earlier reports describe Blackwell-class B200 and B300 systems in China via gray-market sales, state-linked leasing and a Moonshot AI lease contract, and a US official's February 2026 statement that DeepSeek trained a model on Blackwell chips. No Blackwell export licence to China has been authorised.
