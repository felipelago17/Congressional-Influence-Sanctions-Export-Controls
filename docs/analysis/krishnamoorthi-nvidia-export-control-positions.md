## 2. Stated positions this week (paraphrased)

- **Statute over discretion (16 Nov, per diary):** chip-export enforcement should not rest on White House discretion; pass stronger laws through the FY2027 NDAA.
- **Political-influence angle (18 Nov, per diary):** Nvidia's $25m ballroom contribution alongside lobbying to weaken chip rules warrants a congressional inquiry into what was expected in return. Nvidia has said the donation was independent of business interests (CS Monitor, IBTimes, as summarised in search results).
- **Security framing:** compares advanced chips with aircraft carriers and nuclear submarines (diary).
- **Huang (19, 23 Nov):** conduct reckless; wants an investigation of any compromise; calls Nvidia's reinstatement of Huang (announced 23 Nov, praised by the President) "outrageous" and says the investigation is incomplete (RTE).
- **Network and diversion (21, 23 Nov):** wants to know how restricted chips keep reaching China and who helps; op-ed urges permanent legislative safeguards and cites Bloomberg's report of restricted hardware at a Shanghai data centre tied to Alice Huang's network (excerpt only).
- **BIS rule (23 Nov, op-ed title):** calls the new rules "a start" that Congress must make last, i.e. wants statute to back the 16 Nov remote-access rule (see the [RASA note](bis-remote-access-ifr-and-rasa.md)).

## 3. Earlier record in brief (2025 to autumn 2026; committee releases)

Opposed H20/H200 sales and the 15 % revenue-share licences (ECRA fee prohibition; letters with Rep. Meeks; bill with Rep. Kamlager-Dove); sponsored the No Advanced Chips for the CCP Act (with Rep. Bera) and cosponsored the RESTRICT Act; criticised authorisation of Blackwell-class exports to Saudi and UAE firms (20 Nov 2025); raised remote access through South-East Asian data centres at an autumn 2026 hearing; supported the AI Diffusion framework with red lines (Jan 2025).
