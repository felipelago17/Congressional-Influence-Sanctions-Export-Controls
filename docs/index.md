# Congressional Influence on Sanctions and Export Controls

Evidence-based framework mapping **public congressional signals** to **public administration actions** on sanctions, export controls, OFAC guidance, BIS rules, and related financial-crime/regulatory compliance matters.

Elections and party control are inputs, not the subject of this site.

!!! warning "Not legal advice"
    This site is for informational and academic purposes only. It does not constitute legal advice. Always consult qualified export-control and sanctions counsel before taking any compliance decision.

## Core idea

We do not claim “influence.”  
We record documented **signals** and documented **actions**, then grade any connection between them:

- `statutory-mandate`
- `documented-request`
- `proximity-only` (no inference drawn)

See [Linkage Grades](methodology/linkage-grades.md).
