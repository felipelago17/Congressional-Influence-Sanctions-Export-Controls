# Linkage Grades

Any connection between a congressional *signal* and an administration *action* must be recorded in a `linkage` field with one of the following grades. Temporal proximity alone is never sufficient.

| Grade | Definition | Example |
|-------|------------|---------|
| `statutory-mandate` | A statute or binding legal requirement explicitly directs or constrains the action. | CAATSA, ECRA, specific NDAA provision, or other enacted law. |
| `documented-request` | The agency, in an official document, cites Congress, a member letter, a GAO/CRS report, or a hearing record as a reason for or context of the action. | Agency Federal Register preamble, official response to a congressional letter, or cited GAO report. |
| `proximity-only` | The signal and action are topically or temporally related, but no direct documentary evidence of linkage exists. **No inference of causation is drawn.** | Bill introduced near the date of a rulemaking; hearing held on a related topic. |

## Rules

- Prefer the strongest grade that is fully supported by primary sources.
- Always include at least one primary-source URL in the `evidence` field.
- If the grade is `proximity-only`, the `notes` field must state that no inference is drawn.
- Aggregation is by party and committee only. Do not create per-member scorecards.
