# Source Hierarchy

Primary sources are preferred. Secondary sources are clearly labeled and never used as the sole basis for a linkage grade.

## Tier 1 — Primary

- Congress.gov (bill text, actions, roll calls, co-sponsors)
- GovInfo / Congressional Record
- Federal Register (final rules, notices, preambles)
- OFAC official releases, FAQs, general licenses, enforcement actions
- BIS official releases, Federal Register notices, Entity List updates
- Senate LDA REST API (lobbying disclosures)
- Official hearing transcripts or video from committee sites
- GAO, CRS, and OIG reports

## Tier 2 — Official secondary

- Agency press releases that quote or link to Tier 1 documents
- White House or Treasury statements that are later formalized

## Tier 3 — Labeled secondary

- Law-firm alerts (always cited as secondary)
- News reporting (always cited as secondary)

## Rules

- A `statutory-mandate` or `documented-request` grade requires at least one Tier 1 source.
- `proximity-only` may cite Tier 2 or Tier 3, but must state that no inference is drawn.
- Every tracker entry must include at least one URL.
