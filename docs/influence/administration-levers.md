# Administration Levers

Observable channels through which Congress can affect administration actions on sanctions and export controls. All entries are based on public records only.

## Levers tracked

- **Statutory mandates** — enacted law that directs or constrains agency action (ECRA, CAATSA, NDAA provisions, etc.).
- **Appropriations** — funding conditions or report language.
- **Oversight** — hearings, questions for the record, committee letters.
- **Nominee holds** — public holds on Treasury or Commerce nominees.
- **Mandatory reports** — statutory reporting requirements and their deadlines.
- **GAO / CRS / OIG** — reports cited by agencies or committees.

## Status fields used

Every related action records:

- `scheduled`
- `announced`
- `published`
- `in_force`

An announcement is never treated as a published rule.
