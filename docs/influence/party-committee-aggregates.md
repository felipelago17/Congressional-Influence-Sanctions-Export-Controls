# Party and Committee Aggregates

Aggregation is performed by party and by committee only.  
No per-member scorecards are produced.

## What is recorded

- Sponsor party count
- Cosponsor party counts (R / D / other)
- Committee of referral
- Roll-call result with official link (when a vote exists)

## Example (Remote Access Security Act)

- H.R. 2683 (119th): House passed 369-22 on 12 January 2026.
- Referred to Senate Committee on Banking, Housing, and Urban Affairs.

Raw member-level data remains on Congress.gov; this site only stores aggregates.
