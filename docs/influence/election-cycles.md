# Election Cycles

Election results and chamber control are treated as contextual inputs, not causal claims.

## What is tracked

- Change in majority party in the House or Senate
- Change in key committee chairs (Foreign Affairs, Banking, Armed Services, Intelligence)
- Timing relative to the end of a Congress (`died-sine-die` risk)

## Current note (November 2026)

The 119th Congress ends on 3 January 2027.  
Bills that have not been enacted by that date (including the Remote Access Security Act) are marked with sine-die risk. Any reintroduction in the 120th Congress will be recorded as a new signal.

**Midterm result.** Secondary reporting (see [Sanctions Drop #18 reading note](../analysis/sanctions-drop-18-democrats-take-congress.md)) records Democratic control of both chambers from the 120th Congress. This is logged as a contextual input pending confirmation against certified results and the House/Senate organising resolutions in January 2027. The expected effect on this site's subject matter is in oversight capacity (hearings, letters, confirmations, NDAA), not in stand-alone sanctions legislation, which still faces the Senate filibuster and the veto-override threshold.

No inference is drawn from the mere fact of an election.
